The data was always there.
Nobody had time to read it.

Om started Plyxa after running into the same pattern on every coastline he looked at: the signals needed to prevent a collapse already existed — scattered across port ledgers, vessel transponders, echosounders and satellite passes — and no agency had the tooling to assemble them before the season closed.

Plyxa is the answer to that gap. Not a research model that ends as a paper, but a deployable system that fishery departments, enforcement teams and aquaculture operators can point at their own waters and use from the first week.

"Every stock collapse on record left a data trail. The collapse wasn't inevitable — the blindness was."
