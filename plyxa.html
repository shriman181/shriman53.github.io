// The Person Building It
PJ
Pratyush Jena
Founder & CEO, Plyxa
Ocean AI Fisheries Tech Maritime Data AIS Systems

Oceans are the world's most misunderstood data source.

Pratyush Jena founded Plyxa from the conviction that the ocean economy's most solvable crisis isn't environmental — it's informational. Fishery managers make billion-dollar decisions on data that's months old, sourced from trawl surveys that cost millions and sample less than 1% of any managed zone.

With a background spanning ocean data systems, applied machine learning, and maritime operations, Pratyush set out to build the intelligence layer that governments, aquaculture operators, and conservation bodies never had — not as a research project, but as a deployable product.

"Every fish stock collapse in history had a data trail. The collapse wasn't inevitable — the blindness was."

Plyxa is Pratyush's answer to that blindness: a real-time AI platform that turns the ocean's noisy, sparse, multi-source data environment into prescriptive intelligence — delivered directly into the hands of the people making decisions over it.
